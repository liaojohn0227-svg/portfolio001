Project 03

Dreaming Back to 2023

A theatre where the old and the new meet
Year
2023
Type
Theatre
Location
Guling Street, Taipei
Course
Architectural Design
Instructor
Prof. Chen De-Ren

As time moves on, much of history is slowly buried in its long river. People say the old must go before the new can come. Is that a truth, or a fallacy?

This design tries to bring the old era and the new one together, breaking out of the frame of time to create a place where people can dream freely in the river of time.

Dreaming back to 1998? No. Let's dream back to 2023, to a present that has already become the past.
