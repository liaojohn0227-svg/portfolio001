About關於

I grew up outside the mainstream school system. From elementary school I was homeschooled, studying subjects that schools don't usually teach. In the second half of my junior year of high school I began preparing for the university entrance exams, and went on to study architecture at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology.

Growing up, sound, music and the natural environment were the things I spent the most time with. In graduate school, I hope to deepen what I have learned so far, and to put my training to work helping the people and places around me.
