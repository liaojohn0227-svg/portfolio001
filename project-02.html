Project 02

The True Meaning of Love

A church where love draws people near to God
Year
2024
Type
Church
Location
Zhongzheng District, Taipei
Course
Architectural Design
Instructor
Prof. Hsu

What a church means to a city: a place where you can find a God who truly loves you, and whom you can truly love.

A church is a place where love brings people close to God.

This church is designed around love. Like a pair of hands gently opening, it welcomes people from every direction. The entrance stands for openness and acceptance: whatever their background, anyone can step into this quiet place.

Natural light pours down like grace. It lights the space and the heart alike, leading people to feel a holy love beyond words, and a peace within.
