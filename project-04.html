Project 04

NEW Living Culture

Social housing and a concert hall on the Taoyuan art corridor
Year
2024
Type
Social housing
Location
Taoyuan
Course
Architectural Design
Instructor
Prof. Chen

This design starts from the site's location and its surroundings, aiming to connect the Taoyuan District Art Corridor. The corridor runs from the Hengshan Calligraphy Art Center to the Children's Art Museum, and a concert hall placed between them plays a key role in the wider arts village.

The building follows an L-shaped plan, with a stepped form that echoes the Children's Art Museum. This gives every residential floor its own outdoor terraces and green space. The concert hall sits on B1, and changes in floor height draw people down to a platform that links the ground floor with the basement. The southwest part of the site is kept open to preserve the views.

B2–B3: residents' parking, with 115 spaces in total.

B1: the concert hall, with 17 parking spaces for its visitors.

1F: the concert hall lobby, music classrooms, shops and shared facilities for residents.

2F–12F: one- and two-bedroom homes for the young people who make up much of Zhongli District's population.
